Meet Renel

Hi, I’m Renel — the heart behind Renel’s Home Child Care.

Working with children has always been my passion. That’s why I pursued a career in early childhood education. Over the years, I’ve had the joy of supporting children through some of their most important developmental milestones. Every child I meet teaches me something new, and I strive to create an environment where they feel valued, heard, and encouraged to be themselves.

Our Mission

To provide a play-based learning environment where children can explore, grow, and develop essential skills — all while feeling safe and supported.

We also believe that family collaboration is key. Working together with families helps us ensure each child’s unique needs are met with care and understanding.

What We Offer

  • Mixed-Age Group Care

    We provide care for children of varying ages, fostering a community where everyone learns and grows together.

  • Flexible Scheduling

    Our flexible scheduling ensures that your child's care fits around your lifestyle.

  • Mobile Services

    We bring our care directly to you with mobile services for your convenience.

  • Individualized Attention

    Each child receives personalized care tailored to their needs and development.
